The idea

Subscriptions are easy to start and easy to forget.

A free trial here, a team seat there. A few months later, money leaves your account every week for tools nobody opens. SubTrack started from a simple question: what if every recurring charge was visible in one place, before it hits?

Without SubTrack
  • Charges scattered across cards, accounts and inboxes
  • Renewals you only notice on your bank statement
  • Invoices lost in email when it is time to file expenses
  • Prices in dollars, euros and dirhams that never add up
With SubTrack
  • One list of every subscription, its price and its next billing date
  • A reminder days before each renewal, on the channel you choose
  • Invoices pulled from Gmail and attached automatically
  • Every amount converted to your currency with daily rates

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is SubTrack free to use?

Yes. You can create an account and start tracking your subscriptions for free.

What does SubTrack do with my Gmail?

Connecting Gmail is optional. When you do, SubTrack only looks for billing and invoice emails so it can attach them to your subscriptions. You can disconnect at any time from your settings.

Which currencies are supported?

You can record each subscription in the currency you pay in. SubTrack refreshes exchange rates daily and shows totals in your main currency.

How do renewal alerts work?

You create alert rules that choose how many days before a renewal you want a reminder, and where to receive it: Email, Telegram or WhatsApp. Each reminder is sent once per billing cycle.

Can I take my data with me?

Always. Export your subscriptions and invoices as CSV, Excel or JSON whenever you want.
